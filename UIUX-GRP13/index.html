About Tanjung Aru Nelayan

Tanjung Aru Nelayan Beach, located about 8 to 10 kilometers from Labuan town, is a peaceful coastal spot known for its white sandy shores, traditional fishing boats, and scenic mangrove areas.

It's especially popular at sunrise, offering stunning views that sometimes include Mount Kinabalu in the distance. The beach is part of a small fishing village where locals land their catch daily.

On weekends, visitors can enjoy food stalls serving local dishes like ambuyat and fresh seafood. Though charming, the beach faces serious coastal erosion, with some parts narrowing from 30 meters to just 2 or 3 meters.
